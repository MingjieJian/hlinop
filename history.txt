History:

19 Nov 2002: A small bug in HLINOP which allowed the wavelength to go negative if the REFLECT switch was turned on was fixed. Thanks to Kjell Eriksson for reporting it.

4 Jun 2003: I fixed the example files so they actually work;-), the old line file referenced the wrong model (one that is on my machine but not the tarred one!). I also have added compiler options for Intel's Fortran compiler under Linux to the makefile.

26 Aug 2003: Two minor bugs fixed which meant the resonance and Stark broadening in the Lyman alpha red wing was incorrect (bugs meant they were not computed at all).

27 April 2005: Small bug in HLINPROF, meant that log(0) occurred if using the self-broadening grids and interrogating a wavelength beyond the range of the profile grids. Now fixed thanks to Ulrike Heiter.

9 Jul 2008: SYNTH code updated so that it warns, rather than crashes, if one tries to feed it a spherical model. Thanks to Nik.

2 Mar 2011: Small bug for case where XHOLT4000 goes to zero fixed. Thanks to Kjell.

2 Mar 2011: hbop code added.

11 July 2023: Bug fixed in LOGINT routine in HBOP.f.  Thanks to Richard Hoppe. 
12 July 2023: Small improvement to LOGINT - log-linear interpolation performs better than log-log.

17 Dec 2025:  Added hbop_auto.f, and some small changes to hbop.f.  (Note these changes were actually made a year earlier, took some time to post to github;)).

25 Nov 2026: hbop.f - the neutral-perturber (excluded-volume) part of the occupation probability in WCALC is now switchable with CALL HBOP_SET_EXCLUDED_VOLUME(SCALE), which multiplies the excluded volume of neutral H and He (SCALE = 1: Hummer & Mihalas hard spheres as before; SCALE = 0: off). The default is now off. The charged-particle part is unchanged. HBOP recomputes the populations when the setting changes. Test suite and GitHub Actions CI added in tests/ (Mingjie Jian).

25 Nov 2026: hlinprof.f - HLINPROF now uses the full Stehle & Hutcheon (1999) tables (stehle_long.dat, built from the CDS catalogue J/A+AS/140/93 with tools/make_stehle_table.py; corrected Paschen files of 2007): Lyman (n_up 3-30), Balmer and Paschen (to n_up 30), T 2500 K - 1.26e6 K, log n_e 10 - 19.5. Lyman alpha stays with HLINOP. Above a line's last tabulated density the last profile is scaled quasistatically (F0 ~ n_e^(2/3)); below 1e10 cm^-3 the first profile is used; outside the temperature range and for lines without tables HLINPROF returns HLINOP. Padding rows in the CDS files (alpha printed as 0) are removed; a log10(0) at alpha = ALPHA(2) in STEHLE fixed. HSELF_PDWIDTH, HE_PDWIDTH, RAD_WIDTH and ALI_GRIEM replaced by the versions distributed with SME (SMElib), which take (NLOW, NUP), apply self and He broadening only to the lines they were derived for, and give radiative widths for all lines (RAD_WIDTH header: PB, 12/6/2013). hbop.f - line profiles from HLINPROF by default, with the HLINOP cutoffs (new function HLCUT in hlinop.f); CALL HBOP_SET_LINE_PROFILE(0) restores HLINOP for all lines (Mingjie Jian).

26 Nov 2026: hlinop.f - STARK1 now convolves the normalised ion quasistatic profile with the normalised electron profile (impact Lorentzian with the Lewis-cutoff width plus the quasistatic electron wing) instead of adding the parts, which gave areas of 1.7-2.1 and peaks 3-4 times too high. Chosen over convolving norm(ion + electron quasistatic) with norm(impact) by comparison with the Stark-only profiles of Stehle & Hutcheon (1999; 171 Balmer and Paschen cases, Paschen as a blind test) and Lemke (1997, VCS; 48 Brackett cases): median HWHM and peak within 2-5 per cent, wings within 5-8 per cent. The convolution is done once per line and plasma state by FFT and cached (STARK1C); beyond 40 widths the normalised parts are added. The earlier construction is kept as STARK1L, used for Lyman alpha and with CALL HLINOP_SET_CONVOLUTION(0) (named HLINOP_SET_STARK before 27 Nov 2026). STARK1PARTS returns the parts (line constants cached on N, M and the line centre) (Mingjie Jian).

27 Nov 2026: hlinop.f - HPROFL now convolves the Doppler profile (with fine structure), the Lorentz profile (resonance, van der Waals, natural) and the Stark profile (HPCONV) instead of taking the dominant mechanism inside the core and adding all of them outside it, which added up to 39 per cent to the area where Doppler broadening dominates and left Stark broadening out of such cores. The Doppler and Lorentz parts enter through their analytic Fourier transforms, the Stark profile is sampled (with discrete normalisation) and the result is cached per plasma state. Areas are now 1 to 0.1 per cent; HLINOP and the tabulated profiles join without jumps (H30 table / H31 HLINOP). One switch, HLINOP_SET_CONVOLUTION(MODE), covers STARK1 and HPROFL; MODE = 0 reproduces the code before Nov 2026 bit for bit. Lyman alpha keeps the earlier code (Mingjie Jian).

27 Nov 2026: hbop.f - fixes. (1) The dissolved fraction uses the effective quantum number from the code's own level energies (new function HBNSTAR; IONH, the infinite-mass Rydberg, put the n = 2 dissolution edge 2 A blueward of the series limit) and is computed in vacuum wavelengths (WAVE is in air above 2000 A, as for the lines); the edge now coincides with the series limit at which HLINOP cuts the lines. (2) NBF = 15 (was 6; HBF has Gaunt factors for n = 1-15), so bound-free absorption from n = 7-15 is included; HBOP_AUTO (NBF = 15) works up to the n = 15 edge at 20.5 micron (it stopped at 3.28 micron). (3) Levels whose red-wing taper has underflowed are skipped, which removes Inf/NaN in TOTAL and CONTIN beyond about 5 micron. (4) Line broadening by hydrogen uses the total H I density NH instead of the ground-state population NP(1) (Mingjie Jian).

28 Nov 2026: hlinprof.f - HLINPROF convolves the tabulated profiles (Stark with the thermal Doppler profile of H) with the remaining Doppler broadening (a Gaussian of 1/e half width WAVE0*sqrt(DOP**2 - DOP_thermal**2), e.g. microturbulence, which was ignored for tabulated lines) and with the Lorentz profile of the radiative, self and helium widths (previously added beyond 0.2 A, up to 4 per cent extra area). Done by FFT with analytic transforms of the Gaussian and Lorentzian and cached (HLPCONV); the tabulated profile keeps its own area. The same switch HLINOP_SET_CONVOLUTION covers this; ISELF = 1 and ICONV = 2 keep the earlier treatment. hlinop.f, hlinprof.f - the cached convolutions (STARK1C, HPCONV, HLPCONV) are stored on 1200 instead of 240 logarithmic points (interpolation errors up to 2 per cent in steep shoulders), and the grid size no longer overflows the integer range for extremely narrow components (segmentation fault for Lyman 10 at 1e16 cm^-3) (Mingjie Jian).
